Soil Moisture Rule

Never broadcast dry granular urea or chemical fertilizers into bone-dry soil or standing deep water. Ensure adequate moist root zone.

Split Nitrogen Timing

Splitting urea into 3-4 stages (Basal, Tillering/Squaring, Flowering) boosts nutrient use efficiency from 30% to over 60%.

Zinc & Phosphate Separation

Never mix Zinc Sulphate directly with DAP or SSP in the same spray tank; it precipitates into an unavailable chemical sludge.
