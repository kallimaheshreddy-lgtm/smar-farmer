Our Commitment to Agricultural Safety & Veterinary Ethics

Plant Health: Automated image and symptom screening provides preliminary guidance based on verified agricultural literature. It does not replace field visits by agronomists. We do not prescribe unauthorized chemical cocktails.

Animal Health: Smart Farmer AI does NOT prescribe antibiotics, prescription injections, or schedule drugs. Only licensed veterinarians may diagnose and prescribe medicine for livestock. If an animal shows acute emergency signs, immediately dial 1962 or visit your local veterinary dispensary.
